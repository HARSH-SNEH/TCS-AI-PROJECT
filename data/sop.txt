STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Title: Quality Inspection Process

1. Purpose
To ensure that all products meet the required quality standards before dispatch.

2. Scope
This procedure applies to all production lines and quality inspection teams.

3. Responsibilities
- Quality Inspector: Conduct inspections and record results.
- Supervisor: Review inspection reports and take necessary actions.

4. Procedure

Step 1: Pre-Inspection
- Verify machine calibration status.
- Ensure all measuring instruments are available.
- Check raw materials for compliance.

Step 2: In-Process Inspection
- Conduct visual inspection for defects.
- Measure dimensions using appropriate tools.
- Record findings in the inspection sheet.

Step 3: Post-Inspection
- Segregate defective products.
- Label accepted and rejected items clearly.
- Update inspection records.

5. Defect Classification
- Critical Defect: Affects safety or usability.
- Major Defect: Affects performance.
- Minor Defect: Affects appearance only.

6. Handling Non-Conformance
- Stop production if defect rate exceeds threshold.
- Inform supervisor immediately.
- Initiate corrective action.

7. Documentation
- Maintain inspection logs.
- Store records for future audits.

8. Safety Precautions
- Wear protective equipment.
- Handle tools carefully.
- Follow workplace safety guidelines.

9. Review and Approval
- SOP must be reviewed every 6 months.
- Approved by Quality Manager.
